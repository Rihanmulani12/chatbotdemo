✦ Our Story ✦

Bringing Mumbai's Soul
to Texas

Born from a passion for authentic flavors, Spice Garden was founded by Chef Raj Malhotra — a culinary graduate of Mumbai's premier Institute of Hotel Management.

Every dish is crafted using spices sourced directly from Kerala, Rajasthan, and Kashmir. We believe great food is more than sustenance — it's a journey home.
