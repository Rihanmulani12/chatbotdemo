Our Story

More Than a Meal,
It's a Journey

From the bustling streets of Mumbai to the heart of Austin, Spice Garden was born from a love of sharing India's rich culinary heritage.

Chef Arjun Sharma, raised in a family of generations-old cooks in Rajasthan, brought his grandmother's recipes and a modern touch to create an experience unlike any other.

We source our spices directly from farms in Kerala and Rajasthan, ensuring every bite carries the authentic aroma of the land.
